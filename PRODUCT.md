# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: men who train seriously and want visible size, strength and physique results, and who value being coached on technique and progress. They arrive from Instagram, YouTube or search, usually on a phone, and decide whether to book in-person sessions, buy a program, or start the app.

Secondary: anyone buying the online programs or using the app (the Glute-Max Guide serves a broader audience), and brands looking for fitness content partnerships.

## Product Purpose

levicox.com is the home of Levi Cox Fitness. It turns attention from social content into one of four outcomes, weighted equally:

1. In-person personal training enquiries: Miami, FL now (Elev8tion Midtown, or mobile); Melbourne, VIC from January 2027.
2. Sign-ups to the Levi Cox Fitness app (`/app`), a web app that runs Levi's programs with set logging.
3. PDF program sales through Stan Store.
4. Brand partnership enquiries.

Success means a visitor finds the right offer for them quickly and acts on it.

## Positioning

What competitors can't truthfully claim, all four together:

- **Gymnast's technique.** 13 years of men's artistic gymnastics, then coaching and judging, produce a technique-first, patient approach to progress.
- **Science background.** BSc in Anatomy & Neuroscience plus certifications in personal training and nutrition coaching.
- **Everything tracked.** Every set in coaching is written down and the plan is adjusted from what the client actually lifts.
- **Coach-built app.** The app runs the same programs Levi coaches with, with every day planned, unlike build-your-own loggers such as Hevy or Strong.

## Operating Context

- Visitors mostly come from Instagram (@levi_cox, @levicoxfitness) and YouTube (@levicoxfitness) on phones.
- Contact runs through WhatsApp (+61 404 263 024), Instagram DM and email; there is no booking system on the site.
- Purchases happen off-site on Stan Store (stan.store/levicox).
- The app is used on the gym floor between sets: today's session, last time's weights, rest timer.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML/CSS site on GitHub Pages (`CNAME` → levicox.com). Pages: `/` (main site) and `/app/` (app marketing page). No build step or framework.
- Offers and prices on the site today:
  - Train Like Levi: 12-week, 5-day rotating split, $29 PDF / one-off in app.
  - Glute-Max Guide: four glute workouts, $14 PDF / one-off stack in app.
  - App: free tier (4-day base program, own workouts, history and records); Pro $12.99/month or $99/year.
- The app is a web app added to the home screen; it is not in an app store.
- Open decision: when Melbourne becomes the primary location and how Miami is presented after the move.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Levi Cox Fitness. First-person voice from Levi ("I'm Levi Cox", "Tell me your goal").
- Voice: direct, plain, specific about training method; no hype.
- Assets: logo lockup and mark in `images/v2/` and `images/logo-white.svg`; favicon and app icons at the root.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real training photography of Levi in `images/` and `images/v2/`.
- App screenshots in `app/shots/`.
- Credentials: BSc Anatomy & Neuroscience; personal training and nutrition certifications; 7+ years coaching; 13 years of men's artistic gymnastics, plus coaching and judging.
- Absent: client testimonials, before/after results, client counts, press. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Every page routes a visitor to one clear next step among training, app, programs, or partnerships, without one offer burying the others.
2. Prove expertise with real credentials and method detail, never with invented social proof.
3. Phone first: most visitors arrive from social on mobile and act with a thumb.
4. Technique and tracking are the through-line from in-person coaching to the app; say so consistently.
5. Keep it fast and simple: static pages with no framework.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific standard has been set. Keep tap targets of 44px or more and visible focus states, both already in place.
