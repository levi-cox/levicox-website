Questions

Before you apply.

How is this different from the app?

The app gives you programs and a logger. Coaching adds me: a plan written for you, check-ins, form reviews, feedback and changes as you progress. You get the full app with either plan.

How do we communicate?

Inside the app, in one thread. Messages, voice notes and form-check videos all live there, so nothing gets lost. Replies come within 24 hours on the Weekly plan and 48 hours on Check-in, on business days.

I'm not in Miami. Does that matter?

No. Coaching is fully online and works in any time zone. I'm in Miami now and moving to Melbourne in January, so replies are by business day, not by the hour.

What equipment do I need?

My programs assume a full commercial gym. Tell me what you have access to in your intake and I'll adapt the plan.

Can I switch plans or cancel?

Yes. Message me and I'll move you between plans, or manage your subscription from the app and cancel any time. The first month is refundable within 7 days, and there's no free trial on coaching.

Why only ten spots?

Because I read every check-in myself. If spots are full, apply anyway and I'll let you know when one opens.

Do you offer a call first?

Weekly coach clients can book an optional intro call before they start. Mention it in your application.
