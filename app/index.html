Questions.

Is the app really free?

Logging is free forever. You can log your own workouts, build routines and keep your history and records without paying. Programs are included with membership, which starts with a 7-day free trial.

How does the free trial work?

You get 7 days of full access to every program. A card is required to start. Unless you cancel before the 7 days are up, your membership renews at the price you chose until you cancel. You can cancel anytime from your profile in the app.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep your logged history and records and can keep logging your own workouts for free. You lose access to the programs until you rejoin.

Do I need to follow a program?

No. Skip programs and log your own exercises whenever you like.

Which phones does it work on?

It's a web app. Open it in your phone's browser and add it to your home screen to use it like any other app.

What if the equipment I need is taken?

Every exercise lists swaps that train the same muscles. Pick one and carry on with the session.

How is it different from Hevy or Strong?

Those are great if you build your own routines. This app is built around following Levi's programs, with every day planned for you. You can still log your own workouts if you prefer.

Can I get the programs as a PDF?

Yes. The 4 and 5-day programs, Train Like Levi and the Glute-Max Guide are also sold as one-off PDFs on my Stan Store, with no subscription.
