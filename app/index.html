Questions.

Is the app really free?

Yes. You can follow the free 4-day base program or log your own workouts without paying. Extra programs are optional one-off purchases, or included with Pro.

Do I need to follow a program?

No. Skip programs and log your own exercises whenever you like.

Which phones does it work on?

It's a web app. Open it in your phone's browser and add it to your home screen to use it like any other app.

What if the equipment I need is taken?

Every exercise lists swaps that train the same muscles. Pick one and carry on with the session.

How is it different from Hevy or Strong?

Those are great if you build your own routines. This app is built around following Levi's programs, with every day planned for you. You can still log your own workouts if you prefer.

Can I get the programs as a PDF?

Yes. Every program and Stack is also sold as a PDF.
