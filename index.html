How I coach

Four principles. Every session.

You set the goal

It's your body and your goals. You decide where we're going. I get you there. People stick with plans they choose and understand. That's Self-Determination Theory, the research behind my coaching. So I always explain why.

Technique comes first

Good form protects your joints. It makes every rep count. I coach the movement first. The weight goes up when your technique holds.

We measure progress

I track your weights, reps and how you feel. We keep what works and change what doesn't. You always know where you stand.

Habits that last

Training is a few hours a week. Food, sleep and recovery are the rest. I build them into your plan so your results stay after the session ends.

My story

I competed in men's artistic gymnastics for 13 years, from age 5 to 18, and coached and judged at competitions along the way. Technique has been my focus ever since.

I started personal training in 2019 at Les Mills NZ, Fitness First and Hustle Wellness Club, and I've trained 200+ clients one-on-one. I studied Anatomy & Neuroscience and I'm certified in personal training and nutrition coaching, so I coach from how the body works, not from trends.

In 2022 I became Club Manager at Hustle Wellness Club in Melbourne and grew it from 150 to 450 members in two years. Running a club showed me why people stay consistent and why they drop off.

In February 2025 I moved to Miami and I've coached full-time since. From January I'm back in Melbourne.
