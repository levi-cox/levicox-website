How to choose your program

Two decisions get you there: what you want, and how many days you can train in a normal week.

Pick your goal

Build muscle, get stronger, lose fat, train hybrid for lifting and running, or keep general fitness. If you want more than one, choose the one you want most for the next 8 to 12 weeks.

Be honest about your days

Choose the number of days you will actually train, not the number you hope to. Two days you never miss beat five you half do.

Match your experience

New to lifting, or coming back after a long break? Start with the 2 or 3-day version. The 4 and 5-day programs are written for intermediate lifters.

Or let the quiz do it

Answer three questions and the quiz gives you a match and a step-up option.

Questions about the programs

Which training program should I start with?

Pick the goal that matters most right now, then choose the number of days you can train every week without missing sessions. The 2 and 3-day programs are written for beginners. The 4 and 5-day programs are written for intermediate lifters. The quiz on this site matches you in about a minute.

What is the difference between the PDF and the app?

The PDFs are one-time purchases on my Stan Store that open on any phone, and the 4 and 5-day programs cost $29 each. The Levi Cox Fitness app lets you log every set in one place, and membership includes every program. It starts with a 7-day free trial, then $69 a year, $33.99 for 3 months or $16.99 a month. Every program is also in the app with membership.

How long does each program run?

The 2 and 3-day programs run 8 weeks. The 4-day programs run 10 weeks and the 5-day programs run 12 weeks. The last week of every 4 and 5-day program is a deload, so you finish fresh and ready to test your progress.

How long is each workout?

Sessions run from about 50 to 75 minutes, depending on the program. Programs with fewer days tend to have slightly longer sessions because each day covers more. The exact time is listed on every program above.

Can I switch to a different number of days later?

Yes. Every goal has several versions, so you can finish one and move up to a 4 or 5-day program when your schedule allows. The step-up option at the end of the quiz shows you the next version for your goal.

Where can I see how to do the exercises?

I post free exercise demos on my YouTube channel, @levicoxfitness, so you can check your form on any movement in your program.
